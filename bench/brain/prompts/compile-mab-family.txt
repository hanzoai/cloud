You compile a question into a lookup plan over a fact store. The store holds facts of these relations, each with the sentence template it was written in (S = subject, O = object):

capital: "The capital of S is O."
head_of_gov: "The name of the current head of the S government is O."
head_of_state: "The name of the current head of state in S is O."
official_lang: "The official language of S is O."
author: "The author of S is O."
chairperson: "The chairperson of S is O."
director: "The director of S is O."   (director/manager of an organisation)
ceo: "The chief executive officer of S is O."
headquarters: "The headquarters of S is located in the city of O."
educated_at: "The university where S was educated is O."
employer: "S is employed by O."
producer: "The company that produced S is O."   (manufacturer/producer of a product)
broadcaster: "The original broadcaster of S is O."
original_lang: "S was written in the language of O." / "The original language of S is O."
founding_place: "S was founded in the city of O."
genre: "The type of music that S plays is O."
child: "S's child is O."
occupation: "S works in the field of O."
head_coach: "The head coach of S is O."
office: "The <office name> is O."   (S is the whole office name, e.g. "Prime Minister of Sweden", "Illinois Attorney General")
citizenship: "S is a citizen of O."
continent: "S is located in the continent of O."
spouse: "S is married to O."   (symmetric)
performer: "S was performed by O."
country_origin: "S was created in the country of O."   (country of origin of a sport, band, product, work)
death_place: "S died in the city of O."
birth_place: "S was born in the city of O."
language: "S speaks the language of O."   (languages a person speaks, writes or signs)
position: "S plays the position of O."
work_location: "S worked in the city of O."
founder: "S was founded by O."
sport: "S is associated with the sport of O."   (the sport of a player, a team, a position, a competition)
religion: "S is affiliated with the religion of O."
notable_work: "S is famous for O."
creator: "S was created by O."   (creator of a character or work)
developer: "S was developed by O."

Six relation FAMILIES may stand in a chain where the exact relation is uncertain; the store resolves the member:
language: official_lang | original_lang | language        (which language is spoken, written, official)
origin:   citizenship | country_origin                   (the country a person, sport, club or work comes from)
maker:    founder | creator | developer | producer | author | performer
leader:   head_of_state | head_of_gov | chairperson | ceo | director | head_coach | office
place:    birth_place | death_place | work_location | headquarters | founding_place | capital
work:     notable_work | position | occupation | sport | genre
Use a family whenever the question does not say which member it means; use the exact relation when it does.

A plan is the base entity named in the question, then the chain of relations to apply in order: the first relation is applied to the base entity, the next to that result, and so on. The last relation's object is the answer.
